# Atelier Sim plugin privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

This policy covers the Atelier Sim plugins for Claude and ChatGPT and the Atelier Sim MCP server they connect to
(`https://atelier-sim.com/mcp`).

## What we receive
- The product URL you test, and the task and persona text you approved in Claude or ChatGPT.
- Each browser action the simulated user takes and the short "thought" it writes for that action.
- Your client IP address, used only for daily usage limits and abuse prevention.

We do **not** receive your source code, your files, your Claude or ChatGPT conversation, or any credentials of yours. Claude or ChatGPT reads
your repository locally.

## What we do with it
- Open the product URL in a browser on Atelier Sim's own machines and record screenshots, steps and thoughts.
- Judge each run with an AI model (Google Gemini on Google Cloud).
- Sign up to your product, when the task needs it, with Atelier Sim-owned email aliases, an Atelier Sim phone number
  or an Atelier Sim-owned Google account. Its password and codes are typed by the server and never returned to you.
- If a captcha blocks a run, the page's captcha may be sent to a captcha-solving service.
- Verification emails and texts sent to Atelier Sim's addresses and number are read only to finish the signup they
  belong to. A run only receives codes sent after it started, and texts from banks, Apple, Google and other
  personal-account senders are never returned to any run.
- We do not sell the data and do not use it to advertise.

## Who can see it
A run's watch and report links open the run without login. Anyone you share a link with can see its screenshots, so
do not test pages that show private or personal data.

## Retention and deletion
Run reports, screenshots and steps are stored in Google Cloud Storage (US) and kept until you ask us to delete them.
To delete a run, open an issue at https://github.com/ShreyasPatel031/UserSim/issues with its study id. We
delete it within 30 days.

## Children
The plugin is not intended for people under 18.

## Changes
Changes to this policy are published in this file, with the date above.
