# Personal activity simulator — synthetic fixture

> **Synthetic data.** Generated by `synth.py`; numbers say the pipeline works, not how well it predicts a real person.

## Data and split

- 25 observed days, 3420 focused segments, 249 idle gaps, 42 call episodes; 101842 lines read, 3484 duplicates (weekly overlap) dropped, 0 unparseable.
- Time-ordered split: train 2026-01-04 – 2026-01-22 (17 days, of which the last 3 tune blend weights), test 2026-01-23 – 2026-02-01 (8 days).
- Context vocabulary: 19 labels from train (rest → `other`). Timezone America/New_York.
- Test points: 1102 switches (1019 with a next context in the same session).

## Metric definitions

- `next_top1` (next context): top-1 accuracy of the next app/domain.
- `next_top3` (next context): top-3 accuracy of the next app/domain.
- `dwell_log_mae` (time to next switch): mean |log10 predicted - log10 actual| dwell.
- `idle_brier` (goes idle soon): Brier score of P(idle within horizon).
- `first_active_mae` (daily shape): first-active time error, minutes.
- `active_mae` (daily shape): total active time error, minutes.
- `share_tv` (daily shape): total-variation distance of time shares (0..1).
- `call_brier` (calls): Brier score of P(call) per slot.
- `idle_auc`, `call_auc`: ranking quality of the probabilities (0.5 = chance).
- `calls_per_day_err`: |expected call slots − actual call slots| per day.
- Loss diff < 0 means the simulator has lower loss than the best baseline; for accuracies the loss is 1 − accuracy.
