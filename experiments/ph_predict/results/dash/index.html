Product Hunt top-list predictor

Backtest over — daily lists, — weekly lists and — monthly lists (— to —). Each month is predicted by a model trained only on earlier months, and no launch's own votes, comments or ranks are used. Who posts the launch (the hunter's audience and track record) carries most of the signal; the pitch, homepage and LLM judgements add little on top.

Recall@k = share of the real top k that the predicted top k contains, averaged over lists; ranges are 95% bootstrap intervals.
