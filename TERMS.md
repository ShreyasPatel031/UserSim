## What the service does
Atelier Sim runs simulated users, driven by an AI model, in cloud browsers on public web pages you choose, and
reports what they did. Results are simulations, not real user research, and may be wrong.

## Your responsibilities
- Only test pages you own or are allowed to test, and that are publicly reachable.
- Don't use the service to attack, overload, scrape at scale, spam, or break the terms of the sites you test.
- Don't test pages that show private or personal data: run links and screenshots can be opened by anyone with the link.
- Don't try to get around the usage limits or reach the accounts the service uses for signups.

## Accounts the service uses
Simulated users sign up with email addresses, a phone number and a Google account owned by Atelier Sim. You never
receive their passwords or codes. We may close accounts the service created on any site at any time.
