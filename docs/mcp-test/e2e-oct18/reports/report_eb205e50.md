# UserSim study — https://clockify.me

**Clockify is good for signing up, creating client projects, logging time, and checking weekly hours. There was no competitor comparison in this run. The number of steps to complete tasks like signing up, creating a project, and logging time is high.**

Proof: 4/4 runs passed

| Site | Persona | Task | Judge | Proof | Steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product | Dana Kim | Create a free Clockify account with my email (no Google sign-in) and confirm my email so the account is verified. | reached | pass | 15 |
| Product | Dana Kim | Log in to my Clockify account and create a project called Acme Website Redesign for a new client named Acme Co. | reached | pass | 25 |
| Product | Dana Kim | Log in to my Clockify account and log 1 hour 30 minutes for today on the Acme Website Redesign project with the description Homepage wireframes. | reached | pass | 20 |
| Product | Dana Kim | Log in to my Clockify account and find a report showing how many hours I tracked this week and on which project. | reached | pass | 12 |
