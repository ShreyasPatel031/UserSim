Stage fit is scored 0–3 per benchmark: 0 absent, 1 present but predictable from product reputation alone, 2 depends on people in aggregate, 3 depends on people by segment or person with held-out outcomes; shown here as 0–100. Quality criteria are scored 0–2. Overall = (1 − quality share) × weighted stage fit + quality share × weighted quality.
