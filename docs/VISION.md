# Company vision (onboarding for every agent)

Source: Shreyas Patel, 2026-09-28. Read this before planning work. Revisit it often; it overrides older framing.

## Who we are
We are a **research company** whose goal is to build state-of-the-art **user simulation models**.
We work on two technical fronts: **fine-tuning** (training our own social and user simulation models) and **memory systems**.

## Starting product: UserSim
Given any public URL, UserSim:
1. finds the product's competitors,
2. finds matching users for your product and each competitor, meaning DB matching, CRM data, or the matching ICP (ideal customer profile),
3. simulates realistic tasks for those users,
4. runs a validation study across every product, every user, and every task.

UserSim is where we're starting. It is not the whole company.

## Long-term vision
Build excellent user simulation models that other companies plug into their own products. Examples:
- user analytics tools (for example Mixpanel),
- app builders,
- essentially anything user-facing, especially wherever a lot of budget goes to real user testing.

Long-term research goal: match or beat the existing foundation models of human behavior simulation. These are the reference models (track only; don't run, download or evaluate them without Shreyas's OK):
- Centaur
- Socrates
- OSIM-8B (CMU LTI), [cmu-lti/osim-8b](https://huggingface.co/cmu-lti/osim-8b), added 2026-10-09

## What this means for agents
- Research, meaning model quality and memory, is the core. That's why the budget is split 50/50 between R&D and product until product-market fit.
- Judge ideas, leads, and partners by how well they serve simulation-model quality, the UserSim product, or future integration partners (analytics, app builders, user-facing products, heavy user-testing spenders).
- Outreach and CRM tooling is a small part of what we do. Don't ignore those companies, but don't let any one agent's own needs (outreach, ops, tooling) set company direction.
- Good leads and partners: companies whose users or data could improve our models, early users of UserSim, and future platforms our models could plug into.
